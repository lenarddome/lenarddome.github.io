## CPM: a python library for theory-driven modeling in computational psychiatry (Yale MAPs, 2026)

A talk given on 5 February 2026 for the [MAPs: Methods And Primers for Computational Psychiatry and Neuroeconomics](https://medicine.yale.edu/psychiatry/education/programs-and-initiatives/map/support/dome/) series at the Yale Department of Psychiatry.
I introduce the [cpm toolbox](https://devcompsy.github.io/cpm/), a Python library offering a unified, theory-driven framework for computational modelling: multiple task types, models such as reinforcement learning and signal detection, and methods such as hierarchical Bayesian estimation, all behind a modular, interaction-centric interface.

[<i class="fas fa-play"></i> Watch the recording](https://medicine.yale.edu/psychiatry/media-player/cpm-dome-default/) · [<i class="fas fa-file-pdf"></i> Slides](https://medicine.yale.edu/download-file/cf50f68a-9e6c-4204-a26c-9d48519e1e3d/) · [Preprint](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/6jc3t_v1)
