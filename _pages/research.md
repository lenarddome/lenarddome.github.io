Currently, I am working as a Postdoctoral Researcher at [Developmental Computational Psychiatry](https://devcompsy.org) lab at the University of Tübingen, where I **develop scientific software** to streamline and push computational modelling to the forefront of research. I am also a Guest Scientist at the [Max Planck Institute for Biological Cybernetics](https://www.kyb.tuebingen.mpg.de/en).

My primary research focuses on **computational modelling of human heterogeneity** ([Dome and Wills, 2025a](https://psyarxiv.com/ygmcj)).
In addition, I have done effect-centric research on when **intelligent systems fail** with a special focus on the **inverse base-rate effect** - an irrational effect of non-uniform generalization of learned experience (Dome and Wills, [2023](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/0kw671vv), [2025b](https://doi.org/10.31234/osf.io/eskr9)). I have also worked on **computational models of learning and decision-making** in associative learning [(Spicer et al., 2019)](https://doi.org/10.46221/ojepn.2020.8639), and **clustering models of category learning** and their connections to recognition memory [(Dome, Edmunds and Wills, 2021)](https://escholarship.org/uc/item/5r98q3dr).

To top it all, I work hard on **open sourcing science!** Transparency, openness, reproducibility and accessability are at the core of my work culture. I am involved in a variety of open-source science projects.
I am the lead developer of [<i class="fab fa-github"></i> psp](https://github.com/lenarddome/psp), a senior developer of [<i class="fab fa-github"></i> catlearn](https://github.com/ajwills72/catlearn).
